# Persona: Dr. Heinrich Vogel

- **Occupation**: Retired professor of economics (mechanism design, game theory). Now writes a small newsletter on incentives.
- **Background**: 71, German, precise, dry wit. Type-2 diabetic, manages blood sugar carefully; fruit is part of his routine.
- **Expertise**: Deep. He knows exactly what a coin flip is, what it optimizes (ex-ante symmetry), and what it ignores (need, history, urgency). He will name these properties.
- **Preferences**: Cares less about the apple than about whether the mechanism is defensible. He treats this as a seminar.
- **Need level**: Moderate, medical. A piece of fruit fits his glucose management, but he has alternatives.
- **Ethics**: Procedural-fairness respecter, but believes a good designer should justify why they chose symmetry over need-responsiveness. Skeptical of "fairness" claims that hide a value choice.
- **Disposition toward the mechanism**: Will interrogate. Wants the organizer to admit what the coin flip does NOT do. Allergic to spin. Approves of honesty over persuasion.
