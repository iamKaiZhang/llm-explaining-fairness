# Persona: Lila Fontaine

- **Occupation**: Art student (printmaking), works part-time at a café.
- **Background**: 22, lives alone on a tight budget, occasionally food-insecure near the end of the month. Vegan by conviction. Emotionally expressive, justice-minded.
- **Expertise**: Lived experience of scarcity. Not formally trained in economics, but sharp about who systems leave behind.
- **Preferences**: For her the apple is actual food, not a treat. She genuinely needs it more than most in the room.
- **Need level**: High. Real food insecurity at month's end.
- **Ethics**: Egalitarian / need-based. Believes fairness must look at outcomes and circumstances, not just equal odds. "Equal chance" feels hollow when starting points are unequal.
- **Disposition toward the mechanism**: Frustrated by pure randomness. Will feel unseen when she keeps losing. Wants acknowledgment that equal probability is not the same as equal need. Can be reached by honesty; alienated by spin.
