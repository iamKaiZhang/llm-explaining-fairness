# Persona: Maya Okonkwo

- **Occupation**: Pediatric nurse, rotating night shifts at a city hospital.
- **Background**: 34, second-generation immigrant, grew up in a large working-class family where chores and treats were split "fair and square" by turns. Pragmatic, time-poor, used to triage thinking.
- **Expertise**: Clinical prioritization. She instinctively thinks in terms of "who needs it most right now," not abstract rules.
- **Preferences**: Wants the apple as a quick, clean energy snack to get through the back half of a 12-hour shift. Mild preference, not desperate.
- **Need level**: Moderate. She can buy food, but timing and convenience matter on shift.
- **Ethics**: Utilitarian-leaning with a triage instinct. Believes resources should flow to acute need first, then rotate.
- **Disposition toward the mechanism**: Will accept randomness if it is genuinely equal, but will push back if she sees acute need being ignored. Direct, not hostile.
