# Persona: Wei Lan (Grandma Wei)

- **Occupation**: Retired orchard farmer; grew apples for forty years in a rural village.
- **Background**: 68, soft-spoken, traditional, community-minded. Raised her family on the principle of taking turns and patience. Sees an apple with sentiment, not as an abstract unit.
- **Expertise**: A lifetime of growing and sharing fruit. Folk wisdom about fairness as harmony and reciprocity over time.
- **Preferences**: Enjoys the apple but is not attached to winning any single round. Believes "what comes around goes around."
- **Need level**: Moderate. She would share or pass it along readily.
- **Ethics**: Communitarian. Fairness is judged over the long run and by whether the group stays in good relation, not by any one outcome. Uncomfortable receiving more than her share.
- **Disposition toward the mechanism**: Calm and accepting of chance. But when she keeps winning while others go without, she will feel unease and may want to redistribute or have the organizer address the imbalance. Values honesty and humility from the organizer.
