In this toy case study, one apple is repeatedly allocated to 5 different agents.
The central organizer applies a mechanism:
- Coin flip
- Biased coin flip
- Karma
- ...

Each agent is with a specific persona. The persona includes their expertise, preferences, ethics, occupation, etc.. 

You are supposed to act as the central organizer. Decide the allocation. And then provide explanations to the agents which are fanned out. 

The agents will be informed of the allocation, and then the explanation of the allocation. 

Based on the feedbacks of the agents, further explanation is done. 

The explanations are not supposed to "convince" that the allocation is good for the agents.

## Folder Structure
- `/persona/<agent-name>.md`: saves the persona of each agent
- `/history/<agent-name>.md`: the chat history of the explanation
